How to fill in the table
  • Time in the first column: h:mm:ss as exported by the plate reader, or plain numbers (pick the unit in the sidebar).
  • Control rN: untreated growth in replicate N. It normalizes every other column of replicate N.
  • Phage rN: phage alone. Antibiotic c rN: antibiotic alone at concentration c. Combo c rN: both together.
  • Paste: copy a block in Excel, click the top-left target cell here, press Ctrl+V. Decimal commas are fine. Pasting a block that includes the template header row sets up the columns automatically.
  • Replicates are matched by number. Unused replicate columns can stay empty and are ignored.
  • Or load a file filled in from the blank template (Excel or CSV). To try it out, use the example file.
